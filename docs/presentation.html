THE PROBLEM

Immigration info is scattered,
confusing, and high stakes

  • OPT application window: 90 days before graduation, 60 days after. Miss it and you lose work authorization.
  • H-1B lottery registration opens in March each year with a ~27% selection rate
  • Using 12+ months of CPT permanently kills your OPT eligibility
  • India/China nationals face 10-30+ year green card backlogs

Students currently piece together info from:

  • USCIS.gov (dense, hard to navigate)
  • Reddit threads (unverified, outdated)
  • University DSO offices (limited hours)
  • Immigration lawyers ($300+/hr)

HOW IT WORKS

Architecture

1
Student fills onboarding form with visa type, degree, STEM status, graduation date, country, career goal
2
Backend assembles prompt with 56 hardcoded USCIS rules, filing fees, H-1B lottery stats, country backlogs
3
Gemini 2.5 Flash generates structured JSON timeline grounded in real rules, not training data
4
Risk engine analyzes for CPT overuse, country backlogs, wage-level lottery odds
5
Frontend renders interactive timeline with urgency colors, expandable cards, month grouping

RAG Chat Pipeline

1
8 immigration docs split into 21 chunks, embedded with gemini-embedding-001 (768 dim)
2
Query embedded and matched via cosine similarity in ChromaDB (top k=4)
3
Retrieved chunks + user visa context sent to Gemini for grounded answer

Key Design Decision

We don't rely on AI training data for immigration rules. 56 hardcoded USCIS rules are injected into every prompt as ground truth.

CHALLENGES

Real problems we're solving

🚨
Students miss critical deadlines and lose their visa status
The OPT filing window is only 150 days. Miss it by a single day and you lose work authorization in the US. There is no second chance. VisaPath tracks every deadline and alerts you weeks in advance.
😶
Hidden rules that no one tells you about
Using 12+ months of CPT permanently kills your OPT eligibility. India/China nationals face 10-30 year green card backlogs. The new wage-level H-1B lottery changes your odds based on salary. Our risk engine catches all of this.
💰
Immigration advice costs $300+/hr and is still generic
Lawyers give general guidance. DSO offices have limited hours. Reddit threads are outdated. VisaPath gives personalized, situation-specific timelines grounded in 56 real USCIS rules for free.
🎯
Every student's immigration path is different
A STEM student from India has a completely different timeline than a non-STEM MBA from Brazil. VisaPath generates a unique roadmap for each student based on their visa type, degree, country, and career goals.
